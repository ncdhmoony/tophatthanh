# Tổ Phát Thanh | Official Landing Page

https://ncdhmoony.github.io/tophatthanh/

Official landing page of Tổ Phát Thanh - Communications and Events Club of VNU-HCM High School for the Gifted.

---

## Overview

Tổ Phát Thanh Official Landing Page was developed to serve as the official digital presence of Tổ Phát Thanh - Communications and Events Club, one of the longest-standing student organisations at VNU-HCM High School for the Gifted.

This landing page aims to provide information about Tổ Phát Thanh and the club's 25-year history, document the club's activities and culture, introduce the new 2025–2026 Board of Direction and Core Team, recognize the contributions of former members, and promote the recruitment campaign. 

**Credit to Trần Điền Kiệt, who developed the previous generation's recruitment information page, which served as the primary foundation for this project.*
